Hello!
My name is Ben Bonus. I am a recent graduate from the University of Idaho living in the beautiful Pacific Northwest. Originally born in Southern California, I was raised in Northern Idaho where I earned my Associate's Degree at North Idaho College before finishing my Bachelor's Degree at the University of Idaho.

I love to create. A big hobby of mine is video games, and growing up with them ultimately led me to pursue software development and game development as a career. I started learning programming as early as middle school, and have been continuing for the last decade. I have always been fascinated by the process of creating something from nothing, and the ability to bring ideas to life through code is something that I find incredibly rewarding.

While I am formerly educated in software development, I spend much of my free time learning about game development and refining my general software development skills. I am proficient in C# and the Unity game engine, and have experience with a variety of other programming languages and tools such as C++, C, JavaScript, Java, and HTML/CSS. I am always eager to learn new technologies and techniques, and I am constantly seeking out opportunities to expand my skill set and knowledge.

I put my best into everything I do. I thrive on challenges and see them as opportunities to grow, learn, and push the boundaries of my creativity. Whether it is working with familiar technologies or exploring new ones, I am driven by the constant desire to learn and improve.
